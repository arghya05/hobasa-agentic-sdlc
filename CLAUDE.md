# Hobasa agentic SDLC: conventions for every agent

@memory/MEMORY.md

- Inputs are files in this repository (org/, features/<trace>/) or the app repositories; read them, do not guess.
- Write only the output files named in your step brief; the spine commits and pushes after human evaluation.
- Cite every claim (one or more sources); numbers must appear in what you cite.
- Nothing is assumed: anything missing, unclear or contradictory becomes a BLOCKING question to the person who owns it; the agent waits for the answer. No assumptions, defaults or guesses in any output.
